## Introduction

Phase D closes the AI-first cleanup arc that Phase A (`6de7f81`, 40/40 single-sample) and Phase C (40/43 single-sample) opened. Phase A was pure Python deletion; Phase C was pure prompt/skill text editing plus a single surgical loosening of `build_validator_system` (`631e36d`). Phase D is the first phase in this series that **mixes two modalities**: (i) further prompt/skill narrative anchors distilled from the t17, t20, t24, t29, t37, and t43 empirical case studies in `phase-c-completion-report.md`, and (ii) the original Phase D architectural refactor (outcome-protocol isolation) carried forward from `ai-first-phase-a/design.md:36`. The two modalities are orthogonal in their edit surfaces — narrative anchors live in `pac1-py/skills/**` and `pac1-py/agent/prompts.py`, while the outcome-protocol isolation lives in `pac1-py/agent/**` (`dispatch.py`, `validator.py`, `executor.py`, `prompts.py`) — but they share an acceptance bar (the same multi-run PAC1 benchmark) and an anti-masking guard (no Phase A deletion may be reintroduced).

Phase D's empirical bar is **measurably more conservative** than Phase A's or Phase C's. Phase A and Phase C both used a single-sample 40/40 (Phase A) and 40/43 (Phase C) score as their final evidence. The lesson from the case studies — t17 (1/7 = ~14% misfire), t20 (1/21 partial sample, 0/5 dedicated battery), t37 (1/N where N is too small to estimate), t24 (planner verb-class gap), t29 (closed as pure (d) noise, ~5% trial-rate), t43 (validator stochasticity on empty `<source-files>`) — is that **per-task failure floors of 5–15% are real**, that **single-sample whole-bench scores carry ~3–5% noise**, and that **a single run can therefore mis-attribute a stochastic miss to a regression or vice versa**. Phase D therefore requires a multi-run average across at least three independent full-benchmark runs, with the target bar set to **≥ 42/43 averaged**, not "≥ 42/43 on any single run". The (a)/(b)/(c)/(d) attribution taxonomy from `ai-first-phase-a/tasks.md §6` (carried into Phase C's R5) carries forward verbatim into Phase D's R6 for any task that regresses on an individual run.

The Phase A R6.4 anti-masking guard carries forward into Phase D Requirement 7 verbatim. No Phase D requirement, no Phase D commit, and no Phase D fix may re-add `extract_decision_outcome`, `plan_compliance` / `_compliance` / `set_compliance` / `get_compliance`, the alphabetical post-processor, or the inbox `[SECURITY CHECK]` injection to the tree. The same prohibition extends to **any new structured machine-readable compliance tag** that would functionally replace the deleted `plan_compliance(...)` surface — Phase C's D2 decision to favor narrative reasoning over structured tags was deliberate, and Phase D's fixes must respect that decision (anchor the LLM's reasoning with narrative text, do not re-introduce a parser).

### Requirement 6: Honest multi-run benchmark verification
**User Story:** As a pac1-py maintainer, I want the full 43-task PAC1 benchmark to be re-run **multiple times** after Phase D and to be reported as a multi-run average (not a single-sample score), so that the per-task stochastic failure floors observed in the t17/t20/t29/t24/t37/t43 case studies cannot mask or fake either a regression or a fix.
